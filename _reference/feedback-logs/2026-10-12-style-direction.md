# Feedback — style direction across first batch (2026-10-12)

**Projects reviewed:** `training-on-prep` (final), `cardio`, `communication`, `mtvideo`, `not-growing` (drafts v2/v3), `mtvideo-behind` (style trial v3)

## What worked
- **Style A — Team Apexx keyword cards** (training-on-prep + the 4-reel batch): "these look really, really good." Training on Prep approved as-is ("I think this is good").
  - Dark glass cards in the zone below Ash's burned-in captions, red `#d80609` accent bar, Bebas Neue condensed type, word-synced reveals (strike-throughs, slams, meters that drain/recolor, numbered 01–04 reasons, checklists), subtle SFX under the voice.
  - On the first video Ash chose: keyword graphics + SFX only; keep his own captions; no logo intro, no end card/CTA.
- **Style B — behind-the-subject** (mtvideo-behind trial): "this is good… I like this style."
  - Person matte (Robust Video Matting) so giant chrome/red Bebas type, a red brush-X, DOUBT marquee rows, vertical side words and B-roll windows sit *behind* Ash; background darkened with a red glow pulse; logo behind him at the end.

## What didn't
- Style A looks "very consistent with other people using Claude to edit their videos" — fine, but risks looking generic if it's the only look.
- Style B didn't suit MTVideo specifically: lighting isn't great and Ash is "massively in the way" (head/torso fill the frame, so big words get hidden).

## Rules for future videos
- Both styles are approved — learn and reuse both.
- Pick per video: Style B when the footage has headroom/space around Ash and decent lighting; Style A for tight talking-head framing or explainer-heavy content.
- Don't let every video default to the same card look — vary/mix styles so the feed doesn't read as "AI-edited".

## Other notes
- Captions are burned into Ash's own edits — never cover or replace them; keep graphics out of the caption band.
- Filming tip given for Style B: leave about a hand's width of headroom above the head.
- B-roll from his own gym footage is welcome; better clips (lifts, posing, food, check-ins) would make B-roll a bigger part of the look.
