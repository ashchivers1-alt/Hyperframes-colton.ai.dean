# @kingketo — Style Fingerprint

Rolled up across 5 videos (Debw9uGEzex, DfdJu1vAvIc, DfbAZIoiQD0, Dfh7uoKlED8, DfaqC92imCo). Last distilled: 2026-11-10.
Brandon Carter — online fitness coach. Ash's chosen reference: **clean, designed, explainer-first — the opposite of the cheesy "AI edit" look.**

## Signature moves (consistent across videos)
- **Hook pattern:** frame 1 already has the graphic: a headline/news card or physique-photo strip naming the pain/promise in ≤7 words ("STOP Spending *hours* Editing", "7 WAYS TO BECOME UNRECOGNIZABLE", CNN/FOX "TODAY" headline). No intro, no logo.
- **Two layouts, chosen per video:**
  1. **Top card** (talking-head listicles): face full-frame, a wide card above his head, swapping every 2–4s. → `DfdJu1vAvIc`
  2. **Split-screen** (stories/arguments): graphic panel top ~45%, face bottom; caption on the seam; full-screen **punch-ins** on 6–8 key lines. → `Debw9uGEzex`, `DfbAZIoiQD0`
- **Captions:** 1–3 words, ALL CAPS, heavy rounded sans, white with **yellow active word**, dark stroke. Never full sentences.
- **Panels/cards alternate light and dark:** cream/off-white paper (often with faint grid) ↔ charcoal/black grid. This alternation is the single biggest anti-"AI look" move.
- **Typography:** bold sans + one *italic serif* accent word + one **red highlight-box** keyword per headline ("Forgot *his* **Customer**"). Gold/yellow numbers on dark, red on cream.
- **Numbered red pill tags** ("#1 WALK", "#2 TRACK") and small red "• TODAY / • LIVE" pills.
- **Data as proof:** big stats, rings that fill, count-ups, live formulas (200 LB × 10 = 2,000), bar/line charts with **small italic citations**, weekday grids with red ✕, plan-vs-actual bars, 0-CLIENTS tiles, before/after photos.
- **Placeholder "?" boxes that fill in** as he answers; hub/node diagrams for concepts.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts between cards/panels; motion lives *inside* cards (builds, ticks, stamps, count-ups). Occasional pixel-grid wipe. No shaders, no whip-pans.
- **B-roll:** almost never full-screen — lives as photo/clip thumbnails inside cards.
- **CTA:** "Comment KEYWORD" with a fake comment field typing + lead magnet (guide/ebook/live training).
- **Palette:** cream `#F4EFE6`, charcoal `#141414`, red `#E1251B`, gold `#F2C230`, caption yellow `#FFD400`.
- **Energy:** sustained, dense (new visual every 1.5–3s), calm delivery — no shake/slam overload.

## What to steal (ranked by Hyperframes copy-ability for Team Apexx)
1. **Top-card listicle system** — cards above Ash's head (his footage has headroom), red "#N" pill tags, thumbnail + big stat, his burned-in captions untouched. Fits NOT GROWING 4 REASONS, Cardio, Communication directly.
2. **Light/dark card alternation + italic-serif accent + red highlight box** — swap Team Apexx's all-dark cards for cream/charcoal alternation (keep Apexx red `#d80609` as the tag/highlight colour).
3. **Data-proof cards:** live formulas (kcal = bodyweight × N, macros), ring fill for steps/cardio kcal, cited study bar charts, weekday grid ✕, plan-vs-actual bars, client before/afters.
4. **Split-screen + punch-ins** for story/opinion reels (MTVideo-type rants).
5. **Comment-keyword CTA** with fake comment box + lead magnet (e.g. "Comment PREP").
6. Short formats: **RAW vs EDITED / WEEK 1 vs STAGE side-by-side**, **tier lists**, **three-stack** montage.

## Don't copy
- His face/photos, sunglasses, purple LED set, bookshelf, Claude/ChatGPT branding, "ONLINE/EDIT" keywords, his offers.
- Specific study numbers unless they match what Ash actually says.
