# @cameron-das-racing — Style Fingerprint

Rolled up across 3 videos (all 16:9 long-form YouTube, 26–40 min). See `videos/*/analysis.md` for per-video detail. Last distilled: 2026-11-10.

## Signature moves (consistent across videos)
- **Hook pattern:** open in the moment (cold-open crisis with a ticking timer, or a paddock selfie with an "ATTEMPT NO.4" stamp, or a provocative claim). Then the prestige or scale in numbers ("200 DRIVERS", "985 MILES", "$20,000"). Then the honest handicaps stacked up ("just one problem ...", "even more bad news ...", overweight, sick, worst kart). Then the plan or questions. Then "only one way to find out" or the title drop.
- **Motion-graphics density in the hook:** a graphic every 2–3s. Animated maps, giant depth-masked numbers, flag pop-ups, warning tags over heads, rubber-stamp rules, logo slams.
- **Branded in-race HUD system** reused on every onboard:
  - teal glow frame round the video
  - round face chip + name top-left
  - session + big position badge top-right
  - FINAL LAP / CHECKERED FLAG bars top-centre
  - race clock, driver lower-third with stint/time
  - mini battle table bottom-left
  - rival name tags pinned above karts
- **Experiment / goal framing:** explicit numeric goals ("GOAL FOR TODAY 46.0s", "WIN", "top 10") with on-screen goal tracking and a "GOAL COMPLETE" state.
- **Captions:** no karaoke. Big stamp text (white bold with red or green accents, yellow outlined for stakes). Subtitles only for shouts, radio or quiet lines, sometimes on a black card with a speaker tag "[Toby] GO! GO!".
- **Transition rotation:** whip-pans with light leaks, chromatic glitch on tags ("COMING UP", "REPLAY"), smoke bursts on slams, speed ramps, B&W for stamps; the glow frame toggles on for in-kart and off for paddock.
- **Face treatment:** cinema-camera interviews (shallow DOF) plus handheld selfies plus an onboard face cam. A face chip on the HUD.
- **Palette:** teal/cyan #1FE0C8 (HUD), racing red #E10600 (warnings, timers), neon green #39FF6A (goals, replay), yellow #FFE600 (stakes), near-black UI #0B0B0D.
- **Energy profile:** front-loaded and dense hook, then long sustained races with HUD drama, replays and reaction cutaways, then an emotional result.

## What to steal (ranked by Hyperframes copy-ability)
1. **The HUD system as one reusable comp**: glow frame, face chip, session + position badge, FINAL LAP / CHECKERED FLAG bars, battle table. Props per clip (position, session, lap state).
2. **Goal card + goal tracker** for lap-time videos ("GOAL 46.0s", then live chip, then "GOAL COMPLETE" in green).
3. **Depth-masked giant stat text** behind a person (Ash already has the Vision person-matte pipeline).
4. **Animated map journey with a distance counter** (travel to tracks abroad).
5. **"COMING UP" / "REPLAY" glitch tags** to bridge sections.
6. **Rival name tags** pinned above karts; **warning tags** over people's heads.
7. **Stat-as-consequence stamps** and the **rubber-stamp rule** graphic.
8. **Lower-third name tags** with a photo chip for anyone who speaks.
9. **Cold open on the most dramatic moment** with a ticking timer, then rewind.

## Don't copy
- KartSim / KROC / Buckmore branding and official stream graphics, his Astars kit, F4/F3 champion framing, sponsor plugs, cartoon backstory art style.
