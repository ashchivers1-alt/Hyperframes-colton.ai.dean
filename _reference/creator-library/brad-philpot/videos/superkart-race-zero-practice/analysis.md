## Hook (0–60s)
- 0–4s: cold open on the most exciting footage. Onboard of a different kart, a front cam of him driving, then a fast trackside pan of the superkart. No text.
- 5–13s: mid-shot piece to camera trackside with the mic, explaining the challenge (scripted, steady).
- 13–17s: engine detail shots with yellow outlined stats stamped on: "70bhp", "0-60 mph 2.5s". Big numbers sell the danger.
- 17–20s: him climbing into the kart for the first time, awkward (comedy of inexperience).
- 20–28s: helmet-on close-up selfie, with white subtitles running the line "And I'm still entertaining the delusion / that I might be able to finish on the podium / in my first ever go in one of these."
- 28–32s: "Is that realistic?" then cut to the owner with a yellow "KART OWNER" label and a hand-drawn arrow pointing at him.
- 32–45s: piece to camera trackside again: "This is a little bit like every member of the public thinks they could land a plane ... I'm going to see if I can land this plane."
- 45–60s: pushing the kart to the grid, a "WARM-UP" yellow chapter card over his own POV (chest cam) as the engine plays up; trackside of the kart spluttering.
- Spoken hook: "Yesterday, I was busy getting to grips with an insanely fast KZ kart at my childhood racetrack. And today I'm here at a completely different track to drive a completely different kart with double the engine size ... It's going to be the fastest kart I've ever driven ... I've never even sat in one before. I'm getting absolutely no practice. And I'm still entertaining the delusion that I might be able to finish on the podium in my first ever go."
- Script pattern: escalation from yesterday, then the extreme stat, then "never even sat in one", then the ridiculous goal (podium), then a second voice doubting it, then a metaphor ("land the plane"), then an immediate setback (engine problem in warm-up).

## Pacing
- Hook cuts every 1–3s, stats stamped on detail shots.
- Races: long onboard blocks with cutaways to trackside every 10–20s, and a chest-cam / steering-wheel POV mixed with the side-pod cam (camera low by the rear tyre, his boot and the chain in frame). That's a strong speed-sensation angle.
- After each race: helmet-on reaction selfie with subtitles, then the next race.
- Ends with a podium / trophy presentation in the clubhouse.

- White subtitles (bold sans, bottom-centre) on almost every helmet-on or muffled line ("what a kart!", "making him defend.", "gone on the grass, put it in the wall.", "So anyway, I'll catch you guys at the podium.").
