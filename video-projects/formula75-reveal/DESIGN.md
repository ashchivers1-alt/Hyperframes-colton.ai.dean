# DESIGN — Formula 75 reveal (behind-subject)

## Style Prompt
Premium product reveal living *behind* Ash in a walking selfie vlog. The left of the frame drops into a dark,
lit stage; the Train by JP Formula 75 jar rises out of a light slit at the bottom edge, then the ingredient
panel rises beside it. Ash's person matte sits on top of everything, so graphics never cover his face and his
hands pass in front of them. Lit, not colored — the product's own gummy blue/green is the only colour.

## Colors
- `#05080c` — stage darkening behind the graphics (radial, never a flat fill)
- `#6fc3ee` — gummy blue (the "75", light slit, glow) — means *the product*
- `#5fd38a` — gummy green (secondary glow, sparks) — pairs with blue, never on its own
- `#f6f6f6` → `#9aa3ab` — chrome text gradient

## Typography
- Montserrat (matches the product's own "Introducing Formula75" artwork): 300 for kickers, 800 for the title.

## Layout
- 1920×1080 (YouTube timeline). Graphics zone x 60–720 — Ash's head never goes left of x≈770 in this clip.
- The render is placed on V2 of `YouTube – Edit 01` over the last clip (01:01:14:19, 414 frames).

## What NOT to Do
- No graphics over Ash's face or head (the matte keeps him on top; the zone keeps them visible).
- No claims about the product beyond its name and "what's inside" — the review comes later.
- No flat full-frame gradients (H.264 banding) — radial darkening only.
- No hard pops: everything rises from the floor with motion blur and settles.
- Leave the frame clean by the last frame (13.8s) so the next shot isn't affected.
