# DESIGN — Shows clash (behind-subject)

## Style Prompt
Same language as `formula75-reveal` so the video feels like one edit: the left of the frame drops into a dark
side-stage, a light slit at the bottom edge ignites, and the show posters rise out of it *behind* Ash (person
matte on top, hands pass in front). Each show owns its colour — TBJP / NPC 2 Bros = red, PCA = blue — and the
light recolours as he switches shows. When both land on "same weekend… same day" they fan out side by side
and a red stamp slams across them.

## Colors
- `#05080c` stage darkening (radial, left edge)
- `#e0302a` TBJP / NPC 2 Bros red — show 1, and the SAME DAY stamp (the clash)
- `#3f6cf0` PCA blue — show 2
- `#f6f6f6` → `#9aa3ab` chrome kicker text

## Typography
- Montserrat 600 for kickers (matches the Formula 75 piece); Anton for the SAME DAY stamp (impact).

## Layout
- 1920×1080. Graphics zone x 40–760 — Ash's head/shoulders stay between x≈830 and x≈1460 in this section.
- Render covers source 8.0–33.6s of the first clip of `YouTube – Edit 01` → V2 at record 01:00:07:04.

## What NOT to Do
- Never over Ash's face; nothing right of x≈760.
- Show posters as they are (no altered dates/claims); the stamp only states what both posters say (Sat 17 Oct).
- Frame clean between beats (25–30s local gap) and at the very end.
